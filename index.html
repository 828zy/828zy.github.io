✳ About Me

Hi, I’m Ziyue Ma 👋

I am an undergraduate studying Computer Science and Technology in the Top-notch Talent Program at Northwestern Polytechnical University, ranked 1st out of 339 students in my major. My research interests include large multimodal models and world models.

▦ Projects

DUAL EXAM: CONTROLLED EVALUATION OF MULTIMODAL AGENTS AND WORLD MODELS

Dual Exam uses two controlled evaluation tracks to assess multimodal agents’ decision-making and task completion, and world models’ action effects and state consistency. Spanning physical, digital, social, and scientific domains, it analyzes execution evidence to distinguish failures in agents, world models, and their interaction.

Pipelined CPU Design Based on the LoongArch ISA

Designed and verified a five-stage LoongArch CPU, using data forwarding and pipeline stalls to handle hazards. Implemented an AXI interface, CSRs and interrupt/exception handling, a TLB, and two-way set-associative instruction and data caches. Predecode-based timing optimization enabled a 60 MHz clock, with over 10,000 lines of Verilog.
